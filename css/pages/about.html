About Muramba Enterprise Solutions

Muramba Enterprise Solutions is a small transport and delivery business that provides reliable transportation services to individuals and small businesses.


The company specialises in furniture removals, goods delivery and moving services. Its goal is to provide customers with affordable, safe and efficient transport solutions.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and reliable transport service provider.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, safe and efficient transport services to our customers.

Why We Need a Transport Management System

Muramba Enterprise Solutions currently relies on manual record keeping. This can make it difficult to manage customer information, bookings, deliveries and invoices.


The proposed Transport Management System will help digitalise these processes, improve record keeping and make it easier to manage bookings and deliveries.
